DIGITAL ACCESSIBILITY · COURSE PROJECT

Universal Design for Digital Media

An accessible, multi-page learning resource that explains what universal design is, why it matters, and how designers can put it into practice.

Focus
Inclusive design & accessibility

Format
Multi-page website

Approach
Semantic, user-first web design

Project overview

Created for an introduction to digital accessibility class, this site translates foundational universal-design concepts into a clear, approachable resource. It uses the same practices it teaches: semantic HTML, readable typography, consistent layout, and accessible navigation.

What it covers

The resource is structured around three guiding questions: what Universal Design is and how it differs from accessibility; why inclusive design matters ethically, culturally, and practically; and how designers can apply methods and implementation strategies in their own work.

Intent

The goal was to make inclusive-design information easy to understand and usable by a wide range of readers. The visual structure, organization, and writing were intentionally designed around clarity and usability.

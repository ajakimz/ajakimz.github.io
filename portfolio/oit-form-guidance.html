CU BOULDER OIT · CONTENT DESIGN

Form Best Practices & DDS Guidance

Creating reusable, accessibility-informed guidance for clear and user-friendly university forms.

Practice
Content strategy & accessibility

Deliverables
Guide & design-system guidance

Focus
Clear, usable form experiences

Contribution

I created and iteratively revised the Form Best Practices guide, incorporating internal content guidance, design-system resources, CU brand guidance, and accessibility practices. I also developed related design-system style guidance and collected visual examples.

What the guidance covers

The guidance supports labels, form flow, validation, error messaging, privacy, consent, and accessible user-centered language. It consolidates decisions from OIT work into a reusable reference for people designing and updating university forms.

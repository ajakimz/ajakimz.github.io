ABOUT ME

Hello! I’m Aja.

I’m a UX Writer at CU Boulder OIT and a Creative Technology & Design student who cares about making digital experiences clear, useful, and human.

I study Creative Technology & Design at the University of Colorado Boulder, with an emphasis in UX/UI, a UX Certificate, and a Business minor. My work brings together research, content design, and social media strategy.

How I work

Research & content: usability testing, content strategy, accessible content, information architecture, affinity mapping, research synthesis, and presentation design.

Design tools: Figma, FigJam, Qualtrics, Jira, Adobe Creative Cloud, Canva, and G Suite. I also work with HTML/CSS when it supports a design goal.

Outside the work

Outside of class and work, I’m a Captain of the CU Boulder Club Dance Team. I love travel, discovering new cultures, good music, and creative spaces that make you stop and look twice.

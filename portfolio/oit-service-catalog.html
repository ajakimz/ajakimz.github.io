CU BOULDER OIT · UX RESEARCH

Service Catalog Research

Helping understand how university audiences expect OIT service offerings to be organized, named, and discovered.

Methods
Survey design, analysis & synthesis

Tools
Qualtrics, Jira & Sheets

Focus
Information architecture

Contribution

I contributed to research planning and a role-based Qualtrics survey for students, staff and IT professionals, and faculty/researchers. The survey explored category placement, naming, grouping, acronyms, tool names, umbrella categories, and recognition of service offerings.

Research operations

My work included stakeholder-specific spreadsheets, removal of unusable responses, qualitative feedback summaries, research-summary tables, decision documentation, teammate input, and presentation preparation and delivery.

Documented design decisions

Key decisions included one category selection per service offering; optional naming feedback; tool names in parentheses; full names before acronyms; and testing Microsoft 365 as an umbrella-category example. Final placement decisions remain with internal teams.

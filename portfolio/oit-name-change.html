CU BOULDER OIT · UX RESEARCH

Preferred & Legal Name Change

Supporting research into a clearer, more navigable experience for people completing university name-change tasks.

Methods
Usability testing & synthesis

Tools
Figma, FigJam & Jira

Focus
Clarity, navigation & next steps

Contribution

I supported research-plan and script review, participant sign-up and usability sessions, debrief synthesis, current-state Figma updates, issue documentation, comparative research, and recommendation tracking.

What the research surfaced

Clear role labels and numbered steps improved understanding. Participants struggled to locate the help request form and contact information, understand what happens next, identify which systems would update, and find timelines or confirmation.

Recommendations

The team explored role-based headings, numbered steps, simpler navigation, more prominent help and contact information, system-impact lists, timelines, confirmation messaging, and clearly expanded acronyms. Sensitive participant information and internal research details are not included here.
